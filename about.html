About This Site

This site is a reference compilation of Bermuda's shipwrecks and reef dive sites. All information is sourced from the UC San Diego Bermuda 100 Project — a collaboration between UC San Diego, the Bermuda government, and the Bermuda Underwater Exploration Institute to digitally document 100+ shipwreck sites and natural habitats around Bermuda.

Bermuda is known as the shipwreck capital of the world — its surrounding reefs have claimed over 300 vessels over the centuries, from 17th-century wooden galleons to modern freighters.

Data Coverage
  • 35 shipwrecks — from the 17th-century Warwick and Sea Venture to modern wrecks like the Xing Da and Corinthian
  • 12 reef & dive sites — including North Rock, South West Breaker, Cathedral, Snake Pit, and more
  • 20 wrecks with full details from original Bermuda 100 pages or supplementary references
  • 13 wrecks with limited reference data (pages timed out or unavailable at compilation)
  • 12 reef sites with reference data
Bermuda 100 Challenge

The Bermuda 100 Challenge aims to digitally document 100 or more historic shipwrecks and distinct natural habitats in Bermuda's waters using advanced underwater, aerial, and satellite imaging — including 3D models, video, and photography.
