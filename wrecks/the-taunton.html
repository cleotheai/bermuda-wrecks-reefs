The Taunton

Description
Bermuda 100 or . uilt in 1902 in Copenhagen by Burmeister & Wain and initially chartered to the United Fruit Company, the steel-hulled cargo steamer, the Taunton, sailed under the Norwegian flag and featured a powerful triple-expansion steam engine. She was carrying a cargo of coal from Norfolk, Virginia to St. George, Bermuda, when she encountered a misty fog at the entrance to the island’s northern passage through the reefs. The captain posted a lookout, but the Taunton fell victim to Bermuda’s northern reefs on November 24, 1920, after running aground on the North East Breaker reef. 32°28'58.91"N, 64°42'17.64"W 228 feet (69.5 meters) 75,000 cubic feet (2124 cubic meters) 1,329 tons 40 feet (12.2 meters) 300m
