Wreck

The Xing Da

Reference only — details limited
History
This 221-foot-long modern-day 'pirate ship', the Xing Da, was carrying Chinese immigrants to be smuggled into the United States. The ship left a Chinese port in June 1996 en route to Bermuda with a cargo of more than 80 illegal immigrants hoping to reach the U.S. The Xing Da and her crew were boarded by the U.S. Coast Guard. On May 15, 1997, the Xing Da was towed past the seaward edge of Bermuda's northwest facing barrier reef and sent to the bottom where she was scuttled. The ship rests at a depth of 71 feet. There were also allegations the ill-fated Chinese freighter Xing Da may have been carrying illegal drugs in addition to its human cargo.
Current Condition
The Xing Da rests at a depth of 71 feet (21.6 meters) after being scuttled on May 15, 1997. The ship sits upright on the seabed. The 221-foot-long Chinese freighter is a relatively modern wreck compared to Bermuda's historical shipwrecks.
