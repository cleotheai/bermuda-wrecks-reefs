Wreck

The Kate

Reference only — details limited
History
Built in 1874 in Whitby, England, the Kate was an iron-hulled English 'Barkantine' rig. The Kate's huge anchor sits slightly upright on top of the reef south of the main wreckage. On this day in 1878, the iron-hulled English barkantine Kate had a fateful run-in with an unmapped reef off Bermuda's south shore. The wreck was never lost and was always known as a fishing site.
Current Condition
Today the Kate lies in 45 feet of water almost one kilometer from the private Natural Arches Beach on Bermuda's south shore.
