Wreck

The Mary Celestia

Reference only — details limited
History
At the time of its sinking in 1864, the Mary-Celestia was barely two years old. It operated as a blockade runner for the Confederacy to transport banned goods during the American Civil War. On March 21, 1864, the Mary Celestia struck the reef at Archer's Point and sank within 30 minutes. Five people died in the evacuation. The wreck became one of Bermuda's most famous and popular dive sites.
Current Condition
At the time of its sinking in 1864, the Mary-Celestia was barely two years old. It operated as a blockade runner for the Confederacy to transport banned goods during the American Civil War. The wreck lies in 55 feet of water. The paddlewheel, boilers, and engine remain visible. The bow section separated and sit in different locations. The wreck is one of the most popular and well-documented sites in Bermuda.
