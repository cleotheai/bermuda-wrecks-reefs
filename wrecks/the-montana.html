Bermuda 100 360 Video Flythrough of Wreckage Area on Sea Floor or . he side-paddlewheel steamer Montana sank in December 1863 after running into shallow reefs off Bermuda’s northwest coast. Like the Mary-Celestia, the Montana was a blockade runner between England, Bermuda and North Carolina. The sailing ship carried contraband goods, including arms, to and from the Confederacy during the Civil War. The vessel went by three previous names – Nola, Gloria and Paramount – before finishing as the Montana.
