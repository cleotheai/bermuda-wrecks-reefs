Mills Breaker Wrecks
Reference Only
Description
The English iron-hulled cargo sailing vessel, the Beaumarais Castle, was traveling from Calcutta en route to New York when she met her demise on Bermuda’s treacherous Mills Breaker reef on April 25, 1873. Attempts to refloat her failed. One man lost his life and others were poisoned as they inhaled noxious fumes during efforts to get the ship back up.
