Bermuda 100 or . uilt in 1877 by R. Napier & Sons in Glasgow, the steamship originally known as the Balmoral Castle was a new breed of iron-hulled, transatlantic ship for carrying both passengers and light cargo. She was powered by a 383-horsepower triple-expansion engine. Ownership changed hands several times in the late 19th century, including to a Spanish company that renamed her the San Augustin. In the early 1890s the ship was acquired by a Canadian company, the Quebec S.S. Company, Ltd., and renamed the Madiana. 32°28'2.71"N, 64°49'12.00"W 345 feet (105 meters) 25 feet (7.6 meters) 3,045 tons 300m
