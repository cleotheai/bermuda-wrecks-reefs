Wreck

The Madiana

Reference only — details limited
History
Madiana crashed on the reef northeast of North Rock on February 10, 1903. The ship went down, but all hands escaped on life boats. The tiller had wrapped around the rudder post so steam steering failed in heavy ground swell. Madiana was partially salvaged in World War II. The wreck was partially salvaged during the war. The engine is gone, but the twin boilers are still visible, and her stern overhangs into a sand hole. The wreck was originally known as 'The Kingston' until the identity confusion was cleared up.
Current Condition
After spending more than 100 years on the seabed, the Madiana's iron hull is heavily deteriorated. The wreck was partially salvaged in World War II. Her engine is gone, but the twin boilers are still visible, and her stern overhangs into a sand hole. The wreck is located northeast of North Rock in 20 to 80 feet of water.
