North Rock Shipwrecks

History
North Rock Shipwrecks refers to the collection of shipwrecks located on or around North Rock, approximately seven miles from the east end of Bermuda. Coupled with an anchor and ballast, the find lent credence to a story that suggests the remains may be from a ship that arrived at North Rock in 1594 carrying [unknown cargo/purpose]. Many ships have wrecked on or around North Rock.
Current Condition
Multiple wrecks in the North Rock area. North Rock itself is considered one of the crown jewels of Bermuda's reef system with depths ranging from 3 to 30 feet with beautiful coral reefs, massive purple sea fans and fish in crystal-clear waters.
