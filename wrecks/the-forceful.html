The Forceful
Wreck
Site Details
DepthMaximum depth: 60 feet (18.3 meters)
Location32°14'20.94"N, 64°49'39.36"W — about half a mile off Bermuda's South Shore. The Forceful and the King tug boats lie within approximately 100 yards of each other on the seabed, separated by a pristine sandhole where sunrise talons and live helmet conch can sometimes be seen.
Length75 feet (22.8 meters)
Sank2009 — purposefully sunk as part of the Bermuda Intact Wreck Initiative (BIWI), a few days before tropical storm Bertha hit Bermuda.
Protected(Not explicitly stated on the Bermuda 100 page for this site, but standard Bermuda protected dive site protocols apply — buoyed site)
Vessel Type
Tug boat — purposefully sunk as part of the Bermuda Intact Wreck Initiative (BIWI). Larger of the two tug boats at this location (the other being The King, sunk 24 years earlier).
History
The Forceful was purposefully sunk in 2009 as part of the Bermuda Intact Wreck Initiative (BIWI), a few days before tropical storm Bertha hit Bermuda. She is the larger of the two tug boats at this location. The other, the King, was sunk 24 years earlier. The Forceful is also larger than its older neighbor.
Current Condition
The Forceful and the King tug boats are intact and lay within approximately 100 yards of each other on the seabed about half a mile off Bermuda's South Shore, separated by a pristine sandhole where sunrise talons and live helmet conch can sometimes be seen.
