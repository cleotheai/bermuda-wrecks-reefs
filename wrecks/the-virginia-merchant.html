The Virginia Merchant
Reference Only
Description
The English wooden sailing ship, the Virginia Merchant, was owned by the Virginia Company and under the command of Captain Robert Burke. The ship was carrying 179 passengers and crew from Plymouth, England via Bermuda to Jamestown, Virginia. She had just left Castle Harbour when she ran aground on a submerged reef and just 250 meters off the south shore at Sonesta Beach on March 26, 1661. In the 1600s very few people knew how to swim, and tragically, of the 179 passengers and crew on board, only 10 survived. 32°14'41.86"N, 64°49'42.60"W 45 feet (13.7 meters)
