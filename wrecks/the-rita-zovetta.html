Wreck

The Rita Zovetta

Reference only — details limited
History
The Rita Zovetta is a 360-foot Italian cargo ship. The wreck lies on the seaward side of the reef, adjacent to the Pelinaion wreck, to the south east of St. David's Island, near St. David's Lighthouse.
Current Condition
The stern section of the Rita Zovetta is largely intact, and the propeller is visible despite being wedged into the reef. There are a number of swim-throughs. The wreck lies on the seaward side of the reef. Maximum depth: 70 feet (21 meters). The wreck is a favorite spot for photographers with plenty of colorful fish, especially in spring and fall months. The site accommodates both beginners and more experienced divers.
