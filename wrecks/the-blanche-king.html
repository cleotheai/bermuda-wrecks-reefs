Bermuda 100 360 Video Flythrough of Wreckage Area on Sea Floor or . he Blanche King sank in December 1920 en route from Norfolk to Bermuda. It was carrying a cargo of coal when the classic American schooner became stranded on the reefs off Bermuda’s southern shoreline and sank into a sand hole in 35 feet of water. The ship had an iron frame, wooden sides and two decks, four masts and, distinctly, a huge drop keel (i.e., the keel could be retracted into the ship).
