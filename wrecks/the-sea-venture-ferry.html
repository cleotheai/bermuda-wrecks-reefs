The Sea Venture Ferry
Reference Only
Description
The Sea Venture Ferry derived its name from the historic wreck (see above), but it is relatively new, and located at the Eastern Blue Cut. While not interesting historically, the Ferry offers a new wreck site for divers at a depth of roughly 40 feet. While the historic Sea Venture is a strictly restricted site, the Ferry is now an open, buoyed dive site for divers at all levels. 32 23'.19N, 64 53'.08W 50 feet (15.2 meters)
