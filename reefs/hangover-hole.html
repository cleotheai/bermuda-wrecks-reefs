Reef / Dive Site

Hangover Hole

Description
Like the Gleana wreck nearby, Hangover Hole features exceptional canyons and swim-throughs, as well as a difficult-to-find underwater cave. Because of the difficulty of the dive, experience local dive guides are recommended to make the most of exploring Hangover Hole. The location varies in depth between 10 and 45 feet.
Statistics
Type:Dive site — canyons, swim-throughs and underwater cave
Location:32°14'54.17"N, 64°48'52.56"W — near the Gleana wreck
Depth:10–45 feet (3–13.7 m) — varies in depth
Status / Protection:Unknown
