Reef / Dive Site

Sandy Hole

Description
The Sandy Hole buoyed dive site is adjacent to the sand hole containing the last evidence of the Virginia Merchant wreck site (see the Virginia Merchant wreck). The buoyed dive site is at a depth of 6 to 33 feet (2 to 10 meters). Sandy Hole is a Bermuda Protected Area.
