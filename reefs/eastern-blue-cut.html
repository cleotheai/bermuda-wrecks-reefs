Reef / Dive Site

Eastern Blue Cut

Description
Eastern Blue Cut is a dive site area on Bermuda's reef platform. The Sea Venture Ferry is located at the Eastern Blue Cut. While not interesting historically, the Ferry offers a new wreck site for divers at a depth of roughly 40 feet. The Sea Venture Ferry derived its name from the historic wreck, but it is relatively new.
