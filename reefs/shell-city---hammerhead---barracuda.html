Reef / Dive Site

Shell City / Hammerhead / Barracuda

Description
Shell City is a Bermuda Protected Area, while Hammerhead and Barracuda are both open buoy wreck sites. The sites are variably in between 10 and 25 feet of water.
Statistics
Type:Three related dive sites — Shell City (Protected Area), Hammerhead (open buoy wreck), Barracuda (open buoy wreck)
Location:Bermuda — Shell City / Hammerhead / Barracuda area
Depth:Variably between 10 and 25 feet (3–7.6 m)
Status / Protection:Shell City: Protected Area; Hammerhead and Barracuda: open buoy wreck sites
